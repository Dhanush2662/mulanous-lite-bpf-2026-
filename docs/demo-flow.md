# Demo flow

1. Open the app and select synthetic account `acme-001`.
2. Submit the claim “The SSO rollout is blocked for Acme.”
3. Show a `VERIFY` response citing the open Jira issue, CRM risk note, and recent Slack update.
4. Submit “The export timeout is still blocking Acme.”
5. Show `SUPPRESS` with the resolved Jira issue and meeting note.
6. Submit an unsupported claim, such as “Acme is expanding to Europe next week.”
7. Show `ABSTAIN` and the absence of decisive evidence.

All names, accounts, and records shown in this flow are fictional synthetic inputs. The decision is advisory; a human chooses any follow-up.
